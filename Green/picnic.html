Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you go.

Do I need to pay in advance to reserve a spot?

No, reserving a spot through this form is free. Payment details, if any, are shared separately after confirmation.

Can I change my destination after registering?

Yes, just reach out to us with your registered email and we'll help you update your destination before the picnic date.

Is transportation included?

You can choose your own vehicle, the picnic bus, or public transport while filling out the registration form.

What dietary options are available?

We cater to no-restriction, vegetarian, vegan and Jain preferences — just select yours in the form.
